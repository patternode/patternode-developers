# Patternode for developers

Everything you, or your coding agent, need to integrate with Patternode: how the products fit together from the outside, the API and event contracts, code that runs, the infrastructure on your side, and the onboarding playbook.

## The products

| Product | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Delve | The shards toolkit. A time series in; its segments at every level out, for galleries, single views, live tiles and search. Patternode keeps none of your series. | Available. Extending forming series is in preview |
| Essence | Patterns by precedent: what repeats. Runs on Delve | Planned |
| Sentinel | Anomalies by shape: what moves, and the alarm on it. Runs on Delve | Planned |
| Foresight | Forecasting: what comes next. Runs on Delve | Planned |
