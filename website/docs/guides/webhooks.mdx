# Receiving webhooks

Patternode signs every delivery to the [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com) specification. The event types are on [Events](../events/index.md).

1. Set your endpoint: `PATCH /v1/account {"webhook_url": "https://..."}`. HTTPS only.
2. Get the secret: `POST /v1/account/webhook_secret`. Shown once; put it in your secret store.
3. On each delivery:
   1. Verify the signature over `webhook-id.webhook-timestamp.body`, using the raw body bytes, before parsing.
   2. Reject a timestamp more than 5 minutes from your clock.
   3. Drop a `webhook-id` you have seen. Retries carry the same id.
   4. Answer 2xx within 10 seconds, then do the work from a queue.
   5. Ignore event types you do not know.

During a secret rotation a delivery carries two signatures, space separated. Accept either.
