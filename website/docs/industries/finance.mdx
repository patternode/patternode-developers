# Finance

For quantitative research, backtesting and live trading across many instruments: equities, futures, foreign exchange, crypto and rates.

## Your data, as Delve reads it

| You have | Send | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Trades with exchange or receive timestamps in nanoseconds | Bars built from trades, with `time_unit: "ns"` | Times are kept exactly as sent; JSON carries them as strings |
| Vendor OHLC bars | The bars, as they are | Keep the vendor's bar boundaries |
| Quotes | Bars of the mid, or of the bid and the ask as two series | |
| A second, minute or session with no trade | Nothing: a gap | Never a flat filler bar |
| Spreads or returns that cross zero | The series plus a fixed offset | Remove it when you use the segments |

| Horizon | Bar width that usually fits |
|---|---|
| Intraday execution and signals | 1 second to 1 minute |
| Swing and daily | 5 minutes to 1 hour |
| Research across years | 1 day |

## The plan for the job

| Use | Plan |
|---|---|
| Research and backtests | Production, with jobs reading Parquet or Arrow from your storage |
| Live signals feeding a trading system | Mission Critical, gRPC extensions, callers in the jurisdiction's region or connected privately |
| A fund at full scale, or one whose regulator treats Patternode as a critical ICT third party | Dedicated |

Register your venues' trading hours as blackout periods, so Patternode changes nothing while you trade. Size capacity for the open and the close, when every instrument trades at once.

## Backtest and live agree

Pin the profile version (`standard@2026-11`). The same bars with the same version give the same segments, bit for bit, on every transport, region and day, for at least 24 months. So a backtest's segments are the segments you would have had live, and a model validated on them stays valid until you choose to move version.

## Example

`examples/industries/finance/trades_to_delve.py` reads invented trades for two instruments with nanosecond times, builds one-second bars (a second with no trade is a gap), backtests the day as a finished series and checks a second run is identical, then goes live from the backtest's edge, extending every instrument in one request as each second closes. The edges stay in your store.

```bash
python mock/mock_patternode.py &
PATTERNODE_API_KEY=pnk_live_BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBB python examples/industries/finance/trades_to_delve.py
```
